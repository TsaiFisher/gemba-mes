資產負債表Balance Sheet

路由：/BalanceSheet  |  科目餘額彙總資產負債表Route: /BalanceSheet  |  Balance sheet summarised from account balances

依科目餘額彙總產出資產負債表，檢視財務結構；支援列印/匯出。Summarises account balances into the balance sheet for a view of the financial structure; printing and export are supported.

資產負債表畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Balance sheet screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

查詢Search

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.
