管理報表（RPT）Management Reports (RPT)

跨模組管理儀表板與財務/毛利/委外分析報表A cross-module management dashboard with financial, margin and outsource analysis reports

RPT（Report）模組彙整全系統資料提供決策報表：管理儀表板即時呈現營運 KPI，損益表/資產負債表供財務檢視，毛利與邊際貢獻分析深入產品獲利結構，委外對帳報表勾稽委外加工費與損耗。各報表皆可依期間/維度篩選並匯出。The RPT (Report) module pulls data together from across the system into reports for decision making: the management dashboard shows operating KPIs live, the income statement and balance sheet give the financial view, margin and contribution analysis look into how products actually make money, and the outsource reconciliation report ties out subcontract charges and scrap. Every report can be filtered by period and dimension and exported.

作業流程圖（資料來源 → 報表）Process flow chart (data source → report)
