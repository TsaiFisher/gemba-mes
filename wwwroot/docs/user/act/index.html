結帳/IFRS（ACT）Closing / IFRS (ACT)

會計期間鎖、日記帳、月結流程與 IFRS 評估（NRV/ECL/匯率重評）The accounting period lock, the journal, the month-end close and the IFRS assessments (NRV / ECL / revaluation)

ACT（Accounting）模組負責期間管理與結帳：會計期間開關控制全系統過帳（已關帳期間一律擋單），管理日記帳彙集自動分錄並支援手開，月結作業以檢查清單引導關帳步驟；並提供 IFRS 評估工具：IAS 2 存貨 NRV 減損、IFRS 9 預期信用損失（ECL）呆帳準備、IAS 21 期末匯率重評，以及部門實際費用（供期末實際費率重估回刷工單成本）。The ACT (Accounting) module handles period management and closing: the accounting period switch controls posting across the whole system (a closed period blocks every document), the journal gathers the automatic entries and supports manual ones, and month-end close guides you through the steps with a checklist. It also provides the IFRS tools: IAS 2 inventory NRV impairment, IFRS 9 expected credit loss (ECL) provisioning, IAS 21 period-end revaluation, and departmental actual costs (which feed the period-end actual rate re-estimate that restates work order cost).

月結建議順序Suggested month-end order

  1. 確認各模組單據皆已過帳/結案（月結檢查清單逐項檢核）Confirm every module's documents are posted or closed (tick off the month-end checklist item by item)
  2. 部門實際費用輸入 → 實際費率重估回刷（工單成本 Final）Entering departmental actual costs → actual rate re-estimate and restatement (work order cost Final)
  3. NRV / ECL / 匯率重評 評估入帳Booking the NRV, ECL and revaluation assessments
  4. 期間轉 Closing（僅允許調整分錄）→ 關帳 ClosedSwitch the period to Closing (only adjusting entries) → close the period

會計期間鎖是全系統過帳的共同閘口：期間一旦 Closed，該期間的庫存異動、應收付與工單成本皆無法再異動， 必要時須以次期沖銷分錄處理。The accounting period lock is the common gate for posting across the whole system: once a period is Closed, its stock movements, receivables and payables and work order costs can no longer be changed, and anything necessary has to be handled with a reversing entry in the following period.
