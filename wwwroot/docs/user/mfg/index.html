製造執行（MFG）Manufacturing (MFG)

從途程/BOM、MRP 展開、排程派工、現場報工到完工入庫的完整生產流程The full production flow, from routing and BOM through the MRP run, scheduling and dispatch, shop-floor reporting and completion receipt

MFG（Manufacturing）模組涵蓋生產計畫與現場執行：以途程主檔定義製程與 BOM，由訂單經 MRP 展開產生工單與委外採購建議（委外單據作業見The MFG (Manufacturing) module covers production planning and shop-floor execution: routings define the process and the BOM, and MRP explodes sales orders into work orders and outsource purchase suggestions (for outsource documents see 委外管理（OSP）Outsource Management (OSP)模組），透過生產排程與派工作業分派到機台，現場以桌機或行動裝置報工，最後由完工入庫將成品繳庫並計算工單實際成本。模組同時提供工單進度、不良率、稼動率、OEE 等生產分析報表。 module); scheduling and dispatch assign the work to machines, the shop floor reports production from a desktop or a mobile device, and Completion Receipt finally takes the finished goods into stock and works out the work order's actual cost. The module also provides production analysis reports such as work order progress, defect rate, utilisation and OEE.

使用前置Lead time used

  1. BAS：產品主檔（MRP 模式、前置天數）、工作中心（費率、班別群組）、模具BAS: product master (MRP mode, lead days), work centers (rates, shift groups), tooling
  2. MFG：途程主檔（工序 + BOM）、工作日曆、計劃停機MFG: routings (operations + BOM), work calendar, planned downtime
  3. 系統參數：批量政策（LotForLot / MinMultiple）、排程參數System parameters: lot-sizing policy (LotForLot / MinMultiple) and scheduling parameters
