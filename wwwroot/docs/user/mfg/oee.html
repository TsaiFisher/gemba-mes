OEE 設備綜合效率OEE overall equipment effectiveness

路由：/Oee  |  OEE ＝ 稼動率 × 產能效率 × 良率Route: /Oee  |  OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

依國際慣例計算 OEE：稼動率（實際稼動/計畫時間，計畫時間取自工作日曆班別工時）× 產能效率（理想工時/稼動時間，理想工時＝產出 × 標準 UnitTime）× 良率（良品/總產出）。可依機台與期間檢視三因子拆解，找出損失最大來源。Calculates OEE the standard way: Availability (actual run time / planned time, planned time taken from the work calendar shift hours) × Performance (ideal time / run time, where ideal time = output × standard UnitTime) × Quality (good pieces / total output). The three factors can be broken down by machine and period to find the biggest source of loss.

欄位Field說明Guide

計畫工時(分)Planned (min)依工作日曆（正班＋加班）換算的計畫生產分鐘數，為可用率的分母Planned production minutes from the work calendar (regular + overtime), the denominator of availability
稼動工時(分)Run (min)設備實際運轉加工的分鐘數Minutes the machine was actually running
理想工時(分)Ideal (min)總產出 × 標準單位工時，即完全無損耗時應花的分鐘數Total output × standard unit time, i.e. the minutes it would take with no loss at all
總產出 / 良品Total output / Good區間內的總生產數與其中的良品數Total output in the period and the good pieces within it
可用率Availability稼動工時 ÷ 計畫工時，反映設備有多少計畫時間真正在運轉Run hours ÷ planned hours, showing how much of the planned time the machine was really running
表現性Performance理想工時 ÷ 稼動工時，反映運轉期間的速度是否達標Ideal hours ÷ run hours, showing whether the speed during running met the standard
良率Yield良品 ÷ 總產出，反映產出品質Good pieces ÷ total output, a measure of output quality
OEE可用率 × 表現性 × 良率。各比率上限截頂為 100%，避免資料雜訊造成失真Availability × Performance × Quality. Each ratio is capped at 100% so that noisy data does not distort the result
