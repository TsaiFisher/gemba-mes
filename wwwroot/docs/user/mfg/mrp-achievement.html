MRP 達成率MRP attainment

路由：/MrpAchievement  |  MRP 建議落實與準時達成統計Route: /MrpAchievement  |  Statistics on how far MRP suggestions were acted on and delivered on time

檢核 MRP 建議轉單率與工單/採購準時達成率，評估計畫品質與執行力。Checks how many MRP suggestions were converted and how many work orders and purchases were delivered on time, as a measure of planning quality and execution.
