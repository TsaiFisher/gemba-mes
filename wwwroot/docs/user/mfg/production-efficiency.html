生產效率分析Production Efficiency

路由：/ProductionEfficiency  |  標準工時 vs 實際工時效率分析Route: /ProductionEfficiency  |  Standard vs actual hours efficiency analysis

比較報工實際工時與途程標準工時（產出 × UnitTime），計算各工序/機台/人員的生產效率。Compares the actual hours reported with the routing standard (output × UnitTime) to work out the efficiency of each operation, machine and operator.

生產效率分析畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Production efficiency screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.

欄位Field說明Guide

派工數Dispatches區間內該工作中心的派工單數Number of dispatches at the work center in the period
標準工時(min)Std Minutes依途程標準工時計算的應耗工時（準備時間＋數量×單位工時）Hours that should have been used, from the routing standard time (setup time + quantity × unit time)
實際工時(min)Actual Minutes各派工從上工到下工的實際時段合計。生產線多人同時上工時重疊的時段只算一次，下工到再上工之間的中斷不算；沒有上工紀錄的派工不列入Total of the actual clock-on to clock-off time on each dispatch. When several operators work a line at once the overlapping time counts once, and breaks between clock-off and the next clock-on are excluded. Dispatches with no clock-on record are left out
效率%Efficiency%標準工時 ÷ 實際工時；低於 100% 表示實際耗時多於標準，可追查換線、待料或速度損失Standard hours ÷ actual hours; below 100% means it took longer than standard, which can be traced to changeovers, waiting for material or speed loss
