訂單變更決策Order Change Decision

路由：/OrderChangeDecision  |  已投產工單受訂單變更影響的人工決策清單Route: /OrderChangeDecision  |  List of released work orders affected by an order change, for manual decision

訂單減量/取消時：未投產工單由系統自動處理（釋放預留、MRP 以新量重建）；已投產工單則列入本清單，標示 MRP 待同步狀態，由計畫人員決定續產、減量或作廢。When an order is cut back or cancelled: work orders not yet released are handled automatically (reservations released, MRP rebuilds them at the new quantity); released ones appear in this list marked as awaiting MRP sync, and the planner decides whether to carry on, cut back or void.
