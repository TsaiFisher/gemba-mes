採購管理（PUR）Purchasing (PUR)

請購 → 採購 → 進貨（IQC）→ 退貨的對外採購流程The external procurement flow: request → order → receipt (IQC) → return

PUR（Purchasing）模組管理對供應商的採購循環：由請購單彙整需求，轉採購單向廠商下單，進貨單收貨入庫（可觸發 IQC 進料檢驗），品質異常以進貨退出退回；進貨確認後自動產生應付發票（FIN）。委外加工的報價、採購、發退料與收貨已獨立為The PUR (Purchasing) module manages the procurement cycle with suppliers: purchase requests collect demand, purchase orders place it with suppliers, goods receipts bring material into stock (optionally triggering IQC incoming inspection), and quality problems are sent back with a goods return. Once a receipt is confirmed, an AP invoice is created automatically in FIN. Outsourcing quotations, orders, material issues and receipts have moved into their own 委外管理（OSP）Outsource Management (OSP)模組。另提供逾期跟催、供應商交期達成率、採購金額/價格比較等管理報表。 module. It also provides management reports: overdue follow-up, supplier delivery rate, and purchase amount and price comparison.
