委外管理（OSP）Outsource Management (OSP)

委外報價 → 委外採購 → 發料 → 收貨 → 重工／對帳的完整委外加工循環The full outsourcing cycle: quotation → outsource PO → material issue → receipt → rework / reconciliation

OSP（Outsourcing）模組集中管理工序委外加工：以委外報價主檔維護加工廠 × 工序的單價，由 MRP 或人工開立委外採購單， 委外發退料單把原料發往加工廠，完成品以委外收貨入庫並依實際用料計算The OSP (Outsourcing) module manages subcontracted operations in one place: the outsource quotation master holds the price per subcontractor and operation, outsource purchase orders are raised by MRP or by hand, outsource material documents send raw material to the subcontractor, and finished parts come back through an outsource receipt where the actual material usage determines the 超耗扣款Scrap Penalty，加工不良以委外重工單追蹤成本與索賠歸屬。 收貨確認後自動產生委外應付發票（FIN），委外進度追蹤與委外對帳報表（RPT）則提供在外料況與費用勾稽。, while processing rejects are tracked for cost and claim allocation on an outsource rework document. Confirming a receipt creates the outsource AP invoice (FIN) automatically, and outsource progress plus the outsource reconciliation report (RPT) show the material at the subcontractor and reconcile the charges.

委外發料的單位成本在The unit cost of an outsource issue is 發料當下即凍結frozen at the moment of issue（AVCO），超耗扣款依此計算，之後庫存成本變動不影響已發料部分。 委外發料屬形態轉換不入損益，加工費於收貨認列。啟用「庫存簡化模式」時，發料／收貨確認即直接異動，不經倉庫作業逐儲位執行。 (AVCO), which is what the scrap penalty is priced at, so later changes in stock cost do not affect material already issued. An outsource issue is a change of form and does not hit profit or loss; the processing fee is recognized at receipt. With "Simplified Inventory Mode" enabled, confirming an issue or receipt moves the stock directly, without location-by-location warehouse operations.
