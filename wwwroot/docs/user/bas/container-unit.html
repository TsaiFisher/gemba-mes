箱子（容器個體）Boxes (Container Units)

路由：/ContainerUnit  |  重複使用的實體箱子：固定 QR 標籤、目前內容與歷程Route: /ContainerUnit  |  Reusable physical boxes: fixed QR labels, current contents and history

容器主檔定義的是容器種類；這個畫面管理的是一個一個實體箱子。每個箱子有一個固定箱號，印成 QR 標籤貼在箱上長期使用。箱子每次裝貨都會綁到一個新的批號（在完工入庫的分箱入庫指定箱號），之後掃箱子就知道裡面裝什麼、還剩多少、在哪個儲位。The container master defines container types; this screen manages the individual physical boxes. Each box has a fixed box number, printed as a QR label and stuck on the box for long-term use. Every time a box is filled it is linked to a new lot (by giving box numbers in receiving by box in production completion), so scanning the box tells you what is inside, how much is left and which location it is in.

規定：一個箱子不得混裝。一箱只放一個品號的一個批號（生產中的箱子：只放一張工單一道工序的產出）。系統只記「箱子目前綁定哪個批號」，箱內數量直接取該批號在庫存帳上的數量；混裝的話，箱子顯示的內容就不是實際內容。Rule: never mix contents in a box. A box holds one lot of one item only (a box used in production: the output of one operation of one work order only). The system only records which lot a box is linked to and reads the quantity straight from that lot in the stock ledger; if a box is mixed, what the system shows is not what is in the box.

箱子狀態Box status

狀態Status說明Guide
空箱Empty沒有綁定批號，或綁定的批號在帳上已經歸零（領完、出完、報廢完）。可以再裝貨；系統自動判斷，不需要手動回收。Not linked to a lot, or the linked lot is down to zero in the ledger (fully issued, shipped or scrapped). It can be filled again; the system works this out automatically, with no manual return step.
使用中In use綁定的批號在帳上還有數量，這時不能拿來裝別的貨。The linked lot still has stock on hand, so the box cannot be filled with anything else.
在製In process報工時裝進了某張工單某道工序的產出（在製品，還沒入庫）。只有同一張工單可以接著用；工單結案或作廢後自動視為空箱。Production output of one operation of a work order was reported into it (work in process, not yet received). Only the same work order can keep using it; once the work order is closed or voided the box automatically counts as empty.
廠外Off-site箱裡的批號已經跟著出貨、委外發料、借出、樣品出貨、退貨給供應商或跨廠調撥等單據全部出廠，箱子跟貨走了。系統自動判斷；箱子回來後要「回收」才變回空箱，在那之前不能拿來裝貨。被領料、報廢用完的箱子仍在廠內，是空箱。The box's lot has left the plant in full with a shipment, outsource issue, loan, sample shipment, return to supplier or cross-plant transfer, so the box went with the goods. The system works this out automatically; when the box comes back it must be recovered before it is empty again, and until then it cannot be filled. A box whose lot was used up by material issues or scrap is still in the plant and is empty.
停用Inactive損壞或報廢的箱子。停用後不能再裝貨，資料與歷程保留。A damaged or retired box. It can no longer be filled; its data and history are kept.

作業流程Process

建立箱子與列印標籤Create boxes and print labels

1
點「建立箱子」，選容器種類、輸入箱號前綴（預設 BX）與數量，按「建立」。箱號＝前綴-五位流水號（例：BX-00001），接續該前綴目前最大號，全公司不重複。Click "Create Boxes", choose the container type, enter a box number prefix (BX is prefilled) and a quantity, then click "Create". Box number = prefix-5-digit sequence (e.g. BX-00001), continuing from the highest number used with that prefix, unique company-wide.
2
查詢剛建立的箱子，點「列印本頁標籤」（或單一箱子的印表機圖示），把標籤貼在箱子上。標籤的 QR 碼就是箱號，不印內容：內容每次裝貨都不同，掃箱號即可查目前內容。Search for the new boxes and click "Print labels on this page" (or the printer icon on a single box), then stick the labels on the boxes. The label's QR code is the box number and does not show the contents: the contents change each time the box is filled, and scanning the box number shows what is in it now.

裝箱與搬移Fill and move boxes

1
完工入庫勾「分箱入庫」，在「箱號」欄依序掃入要裝的箱子，或按「帶入空箱」自動帶入該種類的空箱。箱號數量要等於箱數；入庫時第 1 箱的批號綁到第 1 個箱號，依此類推。詳見完工入庫。In production completion, tick "Receive by box" and scan the boxes to fill into the "Box numbers" field in order, or click "Fill in empty boxes" to fill in empty boxes of that type. The number of box numbers must equal the number of boxes; on receipt, box 1's lot is linked to the first box number, and so on. See production completion.
2
整箱搬儲位用同廠調撥：在明細的批號欄直接掃箱號，系統會換成該箱目前的批號，並帶出品號、撥出儲位與整箱數量。To relocate a whole box, use a stock transfer: scan the box number into the line's lot field and the box's current lot, item, source location and full quantity are filled in.

進貨上架裝箱、既有庫存裝箱與換箱Packing at put-away, packing existing stock and re-boxing

1
進貨（或借入、銷退等入庫）上架時，在倉庫作業或手機入庫上架的「裝入箱號」掃空箱，批號留空由系統產生新批號（單號-行號-流水號），上架後箱子綁到這一批。自己填批號也可以，但必須是沒用過的批號。When putting away a receipt (or a loan-in, sales return and so on), scan an empty box into "Fill box" in Warehouse Operations or on the phone put-away screen. Leave the lot blank and the system creates a new one (document-line-sequence); after put-away the box is linked to that lot. You can enter your own lot instead, but it must be one that has not been used.
2
已經在庫的散裝貨要裝箱：清單上方點「裝箱」，掃空箱、輸入品號、點選要裝的那份庫存（儲位＋批號）並填數量。系統在同一個儲位把這份庫存換成新批號（數量與成本不變，留下一張已過帳的同廠調撥單），箱子綁到新批號。空箱的內容面板也有「裝箱」。To pack loose goods already on hand, click "Pack" above the list, scan the empty box, enter the product code, choose what to pack (location and lot) and the quantity. The system relabels those goods with a new lot in the same location (quantity and cost unchanged, recorded as a posted stock transfer) and links the box to the new lot. An empty box's details panel also has "Pack".
3
換箱（舊箱壞了、整箱倒到另一個箱子）：在「裝箱」的「來源箱號」掃舊箱，品號、儲位、批號與數量自動帶出；裝好之後舊箱的批號歸零，自動變空箱。要取消裝箱或換箱，作廢那張調撥單即可。Re-boxing (the old box is damaged, or a whole box is emptied into another one): in "Pack", scan the old box into "From box" and its product, location, lot and quantity are filled in; afterwards the old box's lot is down to zero and it becomes empty automatically. To undo packing or re-boxing, void that transfer.

生產中使用箱子Use boxes during production

1
派工報工時在「箱號」欄掃箱子（手機報工掃容器 QR 也可以掃箱號）。報工的合格數就記在這個箱子上，箱子綁到這張工單的這道工序。同一道工序可以分好幾次報進同一箱，數量會累加。When reporting production on a dispatch, scan the box into the "Box No." field (on the phone, the container QR scan also accepts a box number). The good quantity is recorded on that box, and the box is linked to this operation of this work order. One operation can report into the same box several times; the quantities add up.
2
整箱交給下一道工序：下一站報工時掃同一個箱號，箱子就改由那一站接手，數量從那一站的報工重新算起（上一站的已經交出去了）。Hand the whole box to the next operation: when the next station reports, it scans the same box number. The box is taken over by that station, and its quantity restarts from that station's reports (the previous station's output has been handed on).
3
最後一道做完，完工入庫勾「分箱入庫」時掃這些箱子（「帶入空箱」會先帶出這張工單的在製箱），箱子就轉成「使用中」並綁到新的成品批號。After the last operation, scan these boxes when ticking "Receive by box" in production completion ("Fill in empty boxes" lists this work order's in-process boxes first). The boxes then become "In use" and are linked to the new finished-goods lots.
桌機報工掃到裝著別張工單在製品、或裝著庫存批號的箱子，整筆報工會被擋下，請換箱。手機報工（可能是斷線後補送）不擋：報工照收、箱子不綁，畫面會提示原因。在製箱的數量是「放進去時」的紀錄；工序間只拿走一部分的話，系統不追蹤，請以「清空」或換箱處理。If a desktop report scans a box that already holds some other work order's work in process, or an inventory lot, the whole report is rejected; swap in an empty box. A phone report (which may be resent after a lost connection) is not rejected: the report is saved, the box is not linked, and the screen shows why. An in-process box's quantity is what was put in; if only part of it is taken between operations the system does not track that, so clear the box or swap in an empty one.

出廠與回收Leaving the plant and coming back

1
整箱出庫時在倉庫作業的出庫面板（或手機出庫下架）掃箱號，系統帶出該箱的批號與數量。出貨、委外發料等單據把這個批號全部出完後，箱子就自動變成「廠外」，內容面板會顯示是跟著哪一張單據、出給哪個對象。When issuing a whole box, scan the box number in the issue panel of Warehouse Operations (or on the phone pick screen) and the box's lot and quantity are filled in. Once a shipment, outsource issue or similar document has issued all of that lot, the box automatically shows as "Off-site", and the details panel shows which document it left with and the party it went to.
2
箱子回來後，在清單上方點「回收」，把箱號一行一個掃進去（或在單一箱子的內容面板按「回收」）。回收後變成空箱，可以再裝。不在廠外的箱子不能回收，會列出原因。When boxes come back, click "Recover" above the list and scan the box numbers in, one per line (or click "Recover" in a single box's details panel). Recovered boxes become empty and can be filled again. A box that is not off-site cannot be recovered, and the reason is listed.
3
跟跨廠調撥到別廠的箱子：目的廠收貨後，在目的廠的箱子畫面「回收」這些箱號。箱子改屬目的廠；箱裡的批號已經收進目的廠，所以直接是「使用中」，之後在目的廠照常使用。Boxes sent to a second plant with a cross-plant transfer: after the destination plant receives the goods, use "Recover" on the destination plant's Boxes screen for those box numbers. The boxes move to the destination plant, and since their lot has been received there they are "In use" straight away and are used there as normal.
4
出貨單、借出、委外發料、退貨、樣品出貨與跨廠調撥的單據面板與列印，會在明細下方列出「出廠箱號」：這張單據出掉的批號對到哪些箱子、各多少，以及箱子回收了沒有。單據本身不必另外填箱號。The panels and printouts of shipments, loans out, outsource issues, purchase returns, sample shipments and cross-plant transfers list "Boxes shipped" below the lines: which boxes the document's lots belong to, how much each held, and whether each box has come back. You do not enter box numbers on the document itself.

查詢與維護Look up and maintain

1
查詢欄可直接掃箱子上的 QR 碼，也可輸入批號或品號。點「內容」看目前內容（品號、批號、各儲位數量、裝箱單號）與最近 50 筆歷程。The search box accepts a scan of the QR code on the box, or a lot or item number. Click "Details" to see the current contents (item, lot, quantity per location, filling document) and the latest 50 history entries.
2
「清空」：實物已經倒出、但帳上那個批號還有數量時使用（例如剩下的改成散裝）。只解除箱子與批號的對應，不動庫存帳。"Clear": use it when the box has been emptied physically but its lot still has stock in the ledger (for example the remainder is now held loose). It only removes the link between box and lot and does not change stock.
3
「停用」：箱子損壞或報廢時使用，之後可再「啟用」。"Deactivate": use it when a box is damaged or retired; it can be reactivated later.
內容面板若提示「同一箱的批號出現在多個儲位」，表示有人只搬了箱內一部分貨到別的儲位，等於從箱子拿出來了，請確認實物。If the details panel says the box's lot is in more than one location, someone relocated only part of the box, which means it was taken out of the box. Check the physical box.
