1. 系統概覽1. System overview

SmartFactory MES 是整合製造執行、採購、庫存、訂單、品質、財務與結帳管理的企業資訊系統， 透過統一的瀏覽器介面提供各部門使用，並支援多廠別、多語系與行動裝置作業。 各模組間單據自動連動：訂單驅動 MRP 生產與採購、進出貨自動產生應收付發票、 所有庫存與帳務異動受會計期間鎖管制，確保帳實一致。SmartFactory MES is an enterprise system that brings manufacturing execution, purchasing, inventory, orders, quality, finance and closing together. Every department uses it through the same browser interface, and it supports multiple plants, multiple languages and mobile devices. Documents flow between modules automatically: orders drive MRP for production and purchasing, receipts and shipments create AP and AR invoices, and every stock and accounting movement is subject to the accounting period lock, so the books and the physical stock stay in step.

2. 登入與登出2. Logging in and out

2-1 登入2-1 Logging in

1
開啟瀏覽器，輸入系統網址（由系統管理者提供）。Open a browser and go to the system URL (your administrator will give it to you).
2
輸入帳號（Email）與密碼，點擊「登入」。Enter your username (email) and password and click "Log in".
3
若帳號有多個廠別，請於下拉選單選擇要使用的廠別，再進入系統。If the account covers more than one plant, choose the plant you want from the dropdown before entering the system.

2-2 登出2-2 Logging out

點擊右上角使用者名稱，選擇「登出」。建議在結束工作或離開座位前務必登出。Click your name at the top right and choose "Log out". Always log out when you finish work or leave your desk.

2-3 兩階段驗證（2FA）2-3 Two-factor authentication (2FA)

若帳號已啟用兩階段驗證（管理員角色為強制啟用），輸入帳密驗證成功後會先進入「輸入驗證碼」步驟，需輸入 Authenticator App 顯示的驗證碼才完成登入；手機版登入流程也支援同一套 2FA 步驟，詳見If two-factor authentication is enabled on the account (it is compulsory for administrator roles), a correct username and password take you to the verification step, and the code from your authenticator app has to be entered before you are logged in; the mobile login uses the same 2FA step — see 手機版操作說明Mobile guide。

3. 畫面共用說明3. Common screen elements

3-1 畫面架構3-1 Screen layout

  • 左側選單The left-hand menu：依模組分組，點選展開子選單進入各功能畫面。: grouped by module; click to expand a submenu and open a screen.
  • 頂部導覽列The top navigation bar：語系切換（中文 / English）、說明文件、使用者資訊。: language switch (Chinese / English), documentation and user information.
  • 主內容區Main content area：列表、查詢、新增/編輯表單。: the list, the query and the add/edit form.

3-2 列表畫面3-2 List screens

  • 上方為Above is 查詢條件列The query bar，設定條件後點「查詢」。, set your criteria and click "Search".
  • 每頁筆數可於查詢條件列右側下拉調整（10 / 20 / 50 / 100 筆），預設 20 筆。The number of rows per page can be changed with the dropdown at the right of the query bar (10 / 20 / 50 / 100); the default is 20.
  • 表格下方固定顯示分頁列：左邊是「第 X / Y 頁，共 N 筆」，右邊是頁碼。只有一頁時仍會顯示筆數。A pager sits below every table: the left side shows "Page X of Y, N records" and the right side the page numbers. The record count is shown even when there is only one page.
  • 列表最右邊的「操作」欄，每個動作都是圖示加文字（例如 編輯、 刪除），不需要靠圖示猜。In the "Actions" column on the right of each row, every action shows an icon together with its label (for example Edit, Delete), so you never have to guess from the icon alone.
  • 點擊欄標題可Click a column header to 排序Sort；再次點擊切換升/降序。; click again to switch between ascending and descending.
  • 報表畫面的明細表也有分頁。分頁只影響明細列，上方的統計卡與合計一律是整筆查詢結果的數字，不會只算當頁。Report screens paginate their detail tables too. Paging affects only the detail rows — the summary cards and totals above always cover the whole result set, not just the current page.
  • 金額與數量的小數位數跟著該筆的幣別與單位走，所以同一欄在不同列可能位數不同；比率、天數、工時則固定位數。Decimal places for amounts and quantities follow each row's currency and unit of measure, so the same column may show different precision on different rows; rates, day counts and hours use a fixed precision.

3-3 側欄表單（Side Panel）3-3 Side panel forms

新增或編輯時，畫面右側會滑出側欄。填寫完成後點「儲存」；若需關閉可點右上角 ✕ 或按Adding or editing slides a panel out on the right. Fill it in and click "Save"; to close it, click the ✕ at the top right or press Esc。

3-4 線上簽核3-4 Online approval

有簽核流程的單據，儲存後狀態為「草稿」，需點「送審」進入簽核流程，核准後才可進行後續作業。A document with an approval flow is saved as a draft; click "Submit" to send it for approval, and only once it is approved can the next step be taken.

4-2 模組手冊入口4-2 Module manuals

基本資料管理（BAS）Master Data Management (BAS)
全系統共用主檔：公司/廠商、產品、倉庫、工作中心、幣別、稅別、班別與單號規則。上線前優先完成設定。The master data shared by the whole system: companies and suppliers, products, warehouses, work centers, currencies, tax codes, shifts and document numbering rules. Set these up first, before going live.
製造執行（MFG）Manufacturing (MFG)
途程/BOM、MRP 展開、生產排程與派工、現場報工、完工入庫與工單成本，及稼動率/OEE 等生產分析。Routings and BOMs, the MRP run, production scheduling and dispatch, shop-floor reporting, completion receipt and work order cost, plus production analysis such as utilisation and OEE.
採購管理（PUR）Purchasing (PUR)
請購 → 採購 → 進貨（IQC）→ 退貨；交期跟催、供應商評分卡與採購價格分析。Requisition → purchase order → goods receipt (IQC) → return; delivery chasing, the supplier scorecard and purchase price analysis.
委外管理（OSP）Outsource Management (OSP)
委外報價與委外採購、發退料出廠、委外收貨與超耗扣款、委外重工與進度追蹤；收貨自動產生委外應付發票。Outsource quotation and purchasing, issuing and returning material to the subcontractor, outsource receipt with scrap penalty, outsource rework and progress tracking; a receipt creates the outsource AP invoice automatically.
訂單管理（ORD）Order Management (ORD)
銷售/內部訂單、出貨、銷退；訂單驅動 MRP；銷售排名/趨勢/交期達成率等分析。Sales and internal orders, shipments and sales returns; orders drive MRP; analysis such as sales ranking, trend and delivery attainment.
品質管理（QCR）Quality Control (QCR)
IQC/IPQC/FQC 三段檢驗（MIL-STD-1916 抽樣）、NCR 不合格品閉環處置、儀器校正與 SPC 製程能力。The three inspection stages IQC/IPQC/FQC (MIL-STD-1916 sampling), closed-loop NCR disposition, instrument calibration and SPC process capability.
庫存管理（INV）Inventory Management (INV)
庫存單據與倉庫作業（上架/揀貨）、盤點、同廠/跨廠調撥、委外/重工發退料與庫存健康度分析。Inventory documents and warehouse operations (put-away and picking), stock counting, in-plant and cross-plant transfers, outsource and rework material issue and return, and inventory health analysis.
財務管理（FIN）Finance (FIN)
進出貨自動產生應收付發票、付款/收款核銷、銀行帳戶餘額與應收付帳齡分析；多幣別匯兌損益。Receipts and shipments create AP and AR invoices automatically, with payment and receipt allocation, bank account balances and AR/AP aging analysis, plus multi-currency exchange gains and losses.

5-1 代理人設定（休假委派）5-1 Delegation (cover during leave)

在「待我簽核」畫面右上角點「代理人設定」，可自行設定休假期間由誰代理簽核，不需管理員協助：Click "Delegation" at the top right of the "Pending my approval" screen to set who approves for you while you are away — no administrator help needed:

1
選擇代理人、開始日期、結束日期，可填寫原因（選填），儲存。Choose the delegate, the start date and the end date, add a reason if you want, and save.
2
設定啟用（勾選）且日期涵蓋今天時立即生效——儲存當下即把「自己既有的待簽單」全部改派給代理人；新產生的簽核單之後也會直接派給代理人，直到設定停用或結束日期已過。It takes effect immediately when it is ticked as active and today falls in the date range — everything already pending in your name is reassigned to your delegate the moment you save, and new approvals go straight to them too, until the setting is switched off or the end date passes.
3
清單下方可暫停（不刪除，方便之後重新啟用）或刪除既有設定；狀態欄顯示「生效中／已排程／已停用／已過期」。Below the list you can pause an existing setting (without deleting it, so it can be switched on again later) or delete it; the status column shows active, scheduled, paused or expired.
只能設定「誰代理我」，無法代別人設定；若自己目前正被別人設為代理人，畫面上方會提示「目前代理」的清單。You can only say who covers for you, not set it up for anyone else; if someone has named you as their delegate, a "currently delegating for" list appears at the top of the screen.

6. 查詢與匯出6. Querying and exporting

  • 各列表畫面均支援多條件查詢（日期範圍、關鍵字、狀態等）。Every list screen supports multiple query criteria (date range, keyword, status and so on).
  • 有「匯出 Excel」按鈕的畫面可將查詢結果下載為 xlsx 格式。Screens with an "Export Excel" button can download the query result as an xlsx file.
  • 列印類報表點「列印」按鈕，系統開啟新視窗供預覽 / 儲存 PDF。For printable reports, click "Print" and a new window opens where you can preview or save a PDF.
  • 作業單據的列印單上，品號、往來廠商／客戶、單位、幣別、稅別、倉庫、儲位、工作中心、機台、科目等代碼一律連同名稱印出（例如「USD 美元」）；名稱取自基本資料，查不到時只印代碼。On document printouts, codes such as product, supplier/customer, unit, currency, tax, warehouse, location, work center, machine and account are always printed together with their names (for example "USD US Dollar"); names come from the master data, and only the code is printed when no name is found.
  • 列印單底部簽核區：「製作」印建單人（單據沒有建單人時印送審人）；「審核／核准」依最近一次送審的簽核紀錄——只有一位核准者時印在「核准」，多位時最後一位印「核准」、其餘依序印「審核」，簽核中則已核准者印「審核」、「核准」留白；代理簽核印「代理人（代 原簽核人）」。沒有簽核紀錄的格子留白供手簽。「日期」印列印當下的時間，採目前廠別的時區。The sign-off area at the bottom of a printout: "Prepared" shows who created the document (or who submitted it for approval when the document has no creator). "Reviewed" and "Approved" come from the latest approval round — a single approver goes in "Approved"; with several, the last goes in "Approved" and the others in "Reviewed", in order; while approval is still in progress, those who have approved go in "Reviewed" and "Approved" stays blank; a delegate shows as "delegate (for original approver)". Boxes with no approval record are left blank for a hand signature. "Date" is the time of printing, in the current plant's time zone.

8. 搜尋手冊8. Searching the manual

  • 手冊每一頁的頂部都有搜尋框，輸入關鍵字即會列出符合的章節；點擊結果會直接跳到該段落，並把關鍵字標示出來。Every page of the manual has a search box at the top. Type a keyword and the matching sections are listed; choosing a result jumps straight to that section with the keyword highlighted.
  • 鍵盤操作：按 / 跳到搜尋框，↑ ↓ 選擇結果，Enter 開啟，Esc 關閉。Keyboard: press / to jump to the search box, ↑ ↓ to move through the results, Enter to open and Esc to close.
  • 搜尋的範圍是目前顯示的語言：切換成 English 之後，請以英文關鍵字搜尋。Search covers the language currently displayed: after switching to 中文, search with Chinese keywords.

9. 常見問題9. FAQ

Q：我送審後找不到單據了？Q: I submitted a document for approval and now I cannot find it. Where is it?
A：送審後單據仍在列表中，以「待審核」狀態顯示。可在查詢條件中選狀態「待審核」查詢。A: A submitted document is still in the list, with the status "pending approval". Select that status in the query criteria to find it.
Q：儲存後畫面出現紅色錯誤訊息？Q: A red error message appears after I save. What does it mean?
A：請依提示訊息修正必填欄位或格式錯誤後重新儲存。A: Fix the required fields or format errors named in the message and save again.
Q：出貨時顯示「庫存不足」？Q: Shipping says "not enough stock". Why?
A：請至「庫存餘額查詢」確認實際餘量，並確認是否有備料單或其他預留佔用了庫存。A: Check the real balance under "Stock Balance Enquiry" and see whether a staging order or another reservation is holding the stock.
Q：過帳時顯示「期間已關帳」？Q: Posting says "the period is closed". What now?
A：異動日期所屬會計期間已由財務關帳。請確認異動日期，或洽財務人員處理。A: The accounting period the transaction date falls in has been closed by finance. Check the date, or ask finance to help.
Q：如何切換語言？Q: How do I change the language?
A：點擊右上角語言圖示，選擇「繁體中文」或「English」即可切換。A: Click the language icon at the top right and choose 繁體中文 or English.
