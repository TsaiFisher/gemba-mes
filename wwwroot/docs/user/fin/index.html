財務管理（FIN）Finance (FIN)

應付/應收發票、付款/收款核銷、銀行帳戶與帳齡分析AP and AR invoices, payment and receipt allocation, bank accounts and aging analysis

FIN（Finance）模組管理收付款循環：進貨/委外收貨確認自動產生應付發票、出貨確認自動產生應收發票（亦可手開），付款/收款作業以核銷（Allocation）沖抵發票並即時更新銀行帳戶餘額。多幣別交易以交易日匯率入帳，結算時自動認列已實現匯兌損益（IAS 21）。帳齡分析協助掌握逾期應收/應付。The FIN (Finance) module manages the payment and receipt cycle: confirming a goods or outsource receipt creates the AP invoice automatically, confirming a shipment creates the AR invoice (which can also be raised by hand), and payments and receipts allocate against the invoices and update the bank account balance immediately. Foreign currency transactions are booked at the transaction-date rate, and realised exchange gains and losses are recognised automatically on settlement (IAS 21). Aging analysis keeps overdue receivables and payables in view.

核心流程Core flow

進貨/委外收貨 →Goods or outsource receipt → 應付發票AP Invoice → 付款作業AP Payment（核銷）→ 銀行餘額更新 (allocation) → bank balance updated
出貨 →Shipment → 應收發票AR invoice → 收款作業AR Receipt（核銷）→ 銀行餘額更新 (allocation) → bank balance updated
逾期監控：Overdue monitoring: 應付/應收帳齡分析AP / AR aging analysis

發票與收付款皆以交易日匯率換算本幣入帳，核銷結算時自動認列已實現匯兌損益（IAS 21）； 所有過帳受會計期間鎖管制，已關帳期間不可異動。Invoices, payments and receipts are all booked in local currency at the transaction-date rate, and realised exchange gains and losses are recognised automatically on allocation (IAS 21); every posting is subject to the accounting period lock and nothing can be changed in a closed period.
