付款作業AP Payment

路由：/APPayment  |  付款單與應付發票核銷Route: /APPayment  |  Payments allocated against AP invoices

建立付款單選擇付款銀行帳戶與待付發票（可多張、部分金額核銷），確認後沖銷發票並扣減銀行餘額。外幣付款結算時自動認列已實現匯兌損益。Create a payment and choose the bank account and the invoices to pay (several invoices and partial amounts are allowed); confirming it settles the invoices and reduces the bank balance. Realised exchange gains and losses on foreign currency payments are recognised automatically on settlement.

作業流程Process

付款Payment

1
點「新增」選供應商與付款帳戶，帶出未結清發票。Click "Add", choose the supplier and the paying account, and the open invoices are listed.
2
分配各發票核銷金額（合計＝付款額），儲存送審/確認。Allocate the amount across the invoices (the total must equal the payment), then save and submit or confirm.
3
確認後發票轉部分/全額結清，銀行餘額同步扣減。Confirming moves the invoice to partly or fully paid and reduces the bank balance at the same time.
4
沖銷錯誤時作廢付款單，發票與餘額自動回沖。If the allocation is wrong, void the payment and the invoice and the balance are reversed automatically.

多付款的退回與抵用Recovering or applying an overpayment

發票全額付清之後才開進貨折讓單（例如退貨給供應商），我們就多付了錢：這張發票的「未結金額」會是負數，在挑發票清單與應付帳齡分析都看得到。用同一張付款單處理，對這張發票填負數的核銷金額：If a purchase debit note is issued after an invoice has been paid in full (for example goods were returned to the supplier), we have overpaid: the invoice's "Outstanding" becomes negative, and it shows up both in the invoice list on this screen and in AP Aging. Handle it with an ordinary payment by entering a negative allocation against that invoice:

1
供應商退款：只對多付的那張發票填負數（例如 −105），「付款金額」會跟著變成 −105；選收到退款的銀行帳戶後儲存、確認。銀行餘額增加，總帳借 銀行存款、貸 應付帳款。Supplier refunds us: enter a negative amount (for example −105) against the overpaid invoice only; "Payment Amount" follows to −105. Choose the bank account the refund arrives in, then save and confirm. The bank balance goes up, and the ledger is debited to Bank and credited to Accounts Payable.
2
抵用下一張發票：同一張付款單裡，新發票填正數、多付的發票填負數（例如 +1000 與 −105），「付款金額」就是實付的 895。Apply it to another invoice: on the same payment, enter a positive amount against the new invoice and a negative amount against the overpaid one (for example +1000 and −105); "Payment Amount" is then the 895 actually paid.
多付款發票的未結金額是負數，核銷金額填負數即為供應商退款An overpaid invoice shows a negative outstanding amount; a negative allocation records the supplier's refund
負數只能填在未結金額為負的發票上，而且不能超過多付的金額；對還沒付清的發票填負數會被擋下。作廢這張付款單會把發票恢復成多付的狀態。已作廢的發票不能再付款核銷。有多付退回的發票，要先作廢退回那張，才能作廢原付款。A negative amount is only accepted on an invoice whose outstanding amount is negative, and it cannot exceed the overpayment; a negative amount on an invoice that is not yet fully paid is rejected. Voiding the payment puts the invoice back to its overpaid state. A voided invoice cannot be settled by a payment. If an invoice has a refund of an overpayment, void the refund before voiding the original payment.

列印Printing

清單每列的 按鈕（需有列印權限）在新分頁開啟含 QR Code 的列印單，可直接列印或另存 PDF。付款單列印含供應商名稱、付款方式、銀行帳戶、幣別與匯率、付款金額與本幣金額，並列出沖銷的發票。The button on each row of the list (print permission required) opens a printout with a QR code in a new tab, ready to print or save as PDF. The payment printout shows the supplier name, payment method, bank account, currency and exchange rate, amount and local amount, and lists the invoices it settles.
