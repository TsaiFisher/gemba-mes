領料／退料Requisition / Return

路徑：/mobile/mfg/requisition.html  |  現場依派工的工單用料申請領料，或把用剩的料退回Path: /mobile/mfg/requisition.html  |  Requesting material for the dispatch's work order on the shop floor, or returning what is left

從開工作業畫面的「領料／退料」進入（有權限才看得到），畫面列出這張派工所屬工單的用料：需求、已發、已退與發料方式。Opened from "Requisition / Return" on the production session screen (shown only with permission). It lists the work order's materials for this dispatch: required, issued, returned and issue method.

領料申請Requisition

1
在要領的料填「申請量」，可加備註，點「送出領料申請」。Enter the "Request qty" for what you need, add remarks if you like, and tap "Submit requisition".
2
系統建立領料單（MR）並送出成待核准；倉庫核准後在「工單發退料」揀料發給你。The system creates a requisition (MR) and submits it for approval; once the warehouse approves, it is picked and issued under "WO Material Issue/Return".

退料Return

1
切到「退料」，只列已發未退的料。填退料量（不得超過已發未退），點「建立退料單」。Switch to "Return": only issued, not-yet-returned material is listed. Enter the return quantity (no more than issued-not-returned) and tap "Create return slip".
2
把料交給倉庫；倉庫在「工單發退料 → 退料」收進儲位後才算退回。Hand the material to the warehouse; it counts as returned once the warehouse receives it into a location under "WO Material Issue/Return → Return".
兩者送出時若剛好斷線，都會先暫存在手機，恢復連線後自動送出（見斷線保護）。If the connection drops just as you submit either one, it is saved on the phone and sent automatically once connected (see Disconnection protection).
