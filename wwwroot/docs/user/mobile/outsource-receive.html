委外收料Outsource Receiving

路徑：/mobile/inv/outsource-receive.html  |  廠商交回委外加工品時，依委外採購單收料Path: /mobile/inv/outsource-receive.html  |  Receiving goods returned by a subcontractor against the outsourcing PO

從倉儲作業首頁的「委外收料」進入。這一步在「入庫上架」之前：先收料，確認後才會出現在待上架清單。Opened from "Outsource Receiving" on the warehouse home page. This step comes before "Putaway": receive first, and the receipt appears in the put-away list once confirmed.

倉儲作業首頁的「委外收料」卡片會顯示待辦筆數：可收料的委外採購單，加上已核准、待確認的收貨單。The "Outsource Receiving" card on the warehouse home page shows the number of open items: outsource purchase orders with quantity still to receive, plus approved receipts awaiting confirmation.

1
輸入或掃描委外採購單號，或從清單點選（只列已確認且還有未收量的單）。Type or scan the outsourcing PO number, or pick it from the list (only confirmed POs with an open quantity are shown).
2
每一行顯示訂購、已收、草稿中與未收量。填「實收量」與批號；簡化模式下另須指定入庫儲位。超過未收量會再確認一次（可能是廠商多交）；能不能超收依廠別參數「委外超收容許率」與「委外超過容許上限時」，超過容許上限時預設只有具「超量核准」權限的主管能收（見廠別管理）。Each line shows ordered, received, in-draft and open quantities. Enter the "Qty received" and lot; in simplified mode also give the location. Exceeding the open quantity asks for confirmation (the supplier may have over-delivered); whether it can be received follows the plant parameters "Outsource over-receipt tolerance" and "Outsource: beyond the tolerance" — by default only a supervisor with the over-quantity approval right can receive beyond the tolerance (see Plants).
2a
廠別開了「委外收料當場驗收」時，每一行另填「不良數」（抽驗發現的不良件數，沒有就填 0）；儲位可不填，用品號類別預設儲位。不良數不可多於實收量。When the plant has "Inspect outsource deliveries at receipt" on, also enter "Defects" on each line (defects found while sampling; 0 if none); the location is optional and defaults to the product category. Defects cannot exceed the quantity received.
3
廠商已全部交完時勾選「這是最後一批」，再點「確認收料」。Tick "Final delivery" when the supplier has delivered everything, then tap "Confirm receipt".
收回品要先品檢：非免驗品號確認後到「入庫上架」只能放待驗倉，再到「進料檢驗」（清單上標「委外」）驗；合格後系統自動轉入一般倉，委外派工的完工量此時才累計。轉入的一般倉就是品號類別的預設倉庫／儲位（手機收料不問倉儲）；要品檢的品號若類別沒設預設倉儲，送出時會被擋下並寫出品號，請先到產品類別設定。Returned goods are inspected first: after confirming, products that are not inspection-exempt can only be put away into quarantine under "Putaway", then inspected under "Incoming Inspection" (marked "Outsource" in the list); once they pass, the system moves them to a normal warehouse, and only then is the outsourced dispatch's completed quantity updated. That warehouse is the product category's default warehouse/location (the phone does not ask for one); if a product that needs inspection has no category default, submitting is blocked with the product named — set it under Product Categories first.
當場驗收時不必上架、也不必再到進料檢驗：不良 0＝合格，直接入一般倉並累計委外派工完工量；有不良（Ac=0）＝整批入待驗倉，依不合格品處置辦理。若同時開了「收貨時自動扣送外料」，不合格批的送外料照樣扣（廠商已用掉），材料成本隨這批貨一起進待驗倉，由後續處置（重工、退回廠商、報廢）決定去向。With inspection at receipt there is no putaway and no separate incoming inspection: 0 defects = pass, stocked into a normal warehouse and added to the outsourced dispatch's completed quantity; any defect (Ac=0) = the whole lot goes to quarantine for nonconformance handling. If "Issue send-out materials at receipt" is also on, the send-out materials for a rejected lot are still deducted (the vendor has used them); their cost goes into quarantine with the lot, and the later disposition (rework, return to vendor, scrap) decides where it ends up.
工序委外的單（畫面顯示「工序委外」提示）不用填儲位：收回的是在製品，不入庫，送出即回寫該工序派工的完工量，該工序的投料同時自動扣。Outsourced-operation orders (the screen shows an "Outsourced operation" note) need no location: what comes back is work in progress and is not stocked; submitting adds it to that operation's dispatch completed quantity and deducts the operation's input materials at the same time.

送出後After submitting

加工單價、幣別與匯率一律取自委外採購單與匯率主檔，手機不輸入金額。依設定有三種結果：The processing price, currency and exchange rate always come from the outsourcing PO and the rate table; no amounts are entered on the phone. Depending on settings there are three outcomes:

  • 已確認，待上架：到「入庫上架」把它放進儲位。Confirmed, awaiting put-away: put it into a location under "Putaway".
  • 已確認並入庫（簡化模式，或開了當場驗收）：庫存已入帳；當場驗收不合格的已入待驗倉。Confirmed and stocked (simplified mode, or inspection at receipt): stock is already posted; lots that failed at-receipt inspection are in quarantine.
  • 已送簽核：委外進貨設有簽核流程時，核准後會出現在本頁上方的「已核准待確認的收貨單」，點「確認」完成。Sent for approval: when receiving has an approval workflow, the receipt appears under "Approved receipts awaiting confirmation" at the top of this page once approved; tap "Confirm" to finish.
確認收料會同時產生應付發票，所以只能在連線時送出；連按兩次不會建出兩張單。Confirming a receipt also creates the AP invoice, so it can only be submitted online; tapping twice does not create two receipts.
