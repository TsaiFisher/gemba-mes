工單發退料WO Material Issue/Return

路徑：/mobile/inv/issue.html  |  倉庫依發退料單揀料出庫，或把現場退回的料收進儲位，一次完成過帳Path: /mobile/inv/issue.html  |  The warehouse picks against material slips, or receives returned material into a location, and posts in one step

從倉儲作業首頁的「工單發退料」進入。上方切換「發料」或「退料」。發料清單包含廠內發料、已核准的領料、委外發料與重工發料；退料清單包含現場建立的退料單。Opened from "WO Material Issue/Return" on the warehouse home page. Switch between "Issue" and "Return" at the top. The issue list holds in-plant issues, approved requisitions, outsource issues and rework issues; the return list holds return slips raised on the shop floor.

倉儲作業首頁的「工單發退料」卡片會顯示待處理筆數（發 N・退 M），與點進去看到的清單一致。The "Work-Order Issue / Return" card on the warehouse home page shows how many slips are waiting (Issue N · Return M), matching the list inside.

發料Issue

1
點一張單據，畫面列出每一行的申請量，以及這個品號目前有庫存的儲位（倉庫／儲位、批號、在手量）。工單若已做過備料，備料儲位會標上「備料」並排第一。Tap a slip. Each line shows the requested quantity and the locations that currently hold the product (warehouse / location, lot, on-hand quantity). If the work order has been staged, the staging location is marked "Staged" and listed first.
2
點選要揀料的儲位（已備料的品號預選備料儲位，並提示「請先從備料儲位領用」），需要時修改「實發」數量。備料儲位不夠這一行時，畫面自動拆成兩筆：備料儲位領可用量、不足的部分從下一個有庫存的儲位領；也可以按「從其他儲位補」自己加一筆、按 ✕ 移除。整箱領時在「整箱領：掃箱號」掃箱子，系統帶出箱子所在儲位與批號，數量為箱內量（不超過這一行還要領的量）。Tap the location you are picking from (for staged items the staging location is preselected, with a reminder to take it from there first) and change the "Issued" quantity if needed. If the staging location does not hold enough for the line, it is split into two entries automatically: the available quantity from the staging location and the shortfall from the next location that has stock. You can also tap "Add another location" to add an entry yourself, or ✕ to remove one. To take a whole box, scan its number into "Whole box: scan box number"; the box's location and lot are filled in, and the quantity is what the box holds (no more than the line still needs).
3
點「確認發料並過帳」：揀料確認與過帳一次完成，庫存扣除、工單已發量累加、預留同步消耗。Tap "Confirm issue & post": picking and posting happen together; stock is deducted, the work order's issued quantity goes up and its reservation is consumed.
實發填 0 的行不會過帳（這次沒揀到）。整張都沒有數量時無法送出。Lines with an issued quantity of 0 are not posted (not picked this time). A slip with no quantities cannot be submitted.
已備料的料要先從備料儲位扣：這張工單在備料區還有的量，發料／領料一定要先從備料儲位領完；備料區不夠的部分才可以從其他儲位領。桌機存檔時、手機送出時與過帳時都會檢查，違反會擋下並說明該從哪個儲位領、要領多少。退料不受影響。Staged material must be taken from the staging location first: whatever is still in the staging area for this work order has to be issued or requisitioned from the staging location; only the shortfall may come from other locations. This is checked when a desktop slip is saved, when the mobile slip is submitted and when it is posted; a violation is blocked with a message saying which location to use and how much. Returns are not affected.

退料Return

1
點一張退料單，在每一行掃描或輸入退回的儲位，確認「實收」數量。Tap a return slip, scan or type the location it goes back into on each line, and check the "Received" quantity.
2
點「確認收料並過帳」：料件以當初發料成本入庫，工單已退量累加。Tap "Confirm receipt & post": the material comes back in at its original issue cost and the work order's returned quantity goes up.

斷線時When the connection drops

待處理單據需要連線才載得出來。送出過帳時若剛好斷線，會先暫存在手機，恢復連線後自動過帳。伺服器在送達當下才檢查庫存與單據狀態，失敗的項目會留在首頁的待送出清單；暫存超過 30 分鐘會改為「待確認」。詳見斷線保護。Pending slips need a connection to load. If the connection drops just as you post, the entry is saved on the phone and posted automatically once it returns. Stock and slip status are checked when it arrives; failures stay in the waiting list on the home page, and entries saved for more than 30 minutes become "Needs confirmation". See Disconnection protection.
