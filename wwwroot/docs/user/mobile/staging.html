備料作業Material Staging

路徑：/mobile/inv/staging.html  |  確認備料單（鎖定庫存），再把料揀移到備料倉Path: /mobile/inv/staging.html  |  Confirm staging orders (reserve stock), then move the material into the staging area

從倉儲作業首頁的「備料作業」進入。清單列出兩種備料單：待確認（已核准的草稿）與待移入（已確認）。備料單由桌機開立並送審。Opened from "Material Staging" on the warehouse home page. The list holds two kinds of staging orders: to confirm (approved drafts) and to stage (confirmed). Staging orders are raised and submitted on the desktop.

倉儲作業首頁的「備料作業」卡片會顯示待確認與待移入的備料單筆數。The "Staging" card on the warehouse home page shows how many staging orders are waiting to be confirmed or moved.

1
待確認的單：點「確認並鎖定庫存」，把工單的軟鎖定轉給這張備料單。可用量不足會當場提示。只能在連線時做。For an order to confirm: tap "Confirm & reserve stock", which moves the work order's soft reservation onto this staging order. Insufficient stock is reported on the spot. Online only.
2
待移入的單：每一行點選要揀料的來源儲位（列出有庫存的儲位，不含備料倉本身），點「揀料移入備料倉」。For an order to stage: tap the source location on each line (locations holding stock are listed, excluding the staging area itself), then tap "Pick into staging area".
移入備料倉後鎖定仍保留，直到發料過帳才釋放，避免備好的料被別張工單用掉。移入送出時若剛好斷線，會先暫存在手機、恢復連線後自動送出。The reservation stays after staging and is released only when the issue is posted, so staged material cannot be taken by another work order. If the connection drops just as you stage, the entry is saved on the phone and sent automatically when it returns.
