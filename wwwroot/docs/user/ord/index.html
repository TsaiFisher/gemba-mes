訂單管理（ORD）Order Management (ORD)

銷售訂單 → 出貨 → 銷退的訂單履行流程與銷售分析The order fulfilment flow — sales order → shipment → sales return — and the sales analytics around it

ORD（Order）模組管理客戶訂單履行：銷售訂單承接客戶需求並驅動 MRP 生產，出貨單揀貨出庫並自動產生應收發票（FIN），品質或商務退回以銷退單處理；內部訂單支援廠內/關係企業需求。另提供逾期跟催、銷售排名/趨勢、交期達成率、退貨率與銷售預測分析。The ORD (Order) module manages fulfilment of customer orders: sales orders capture customer demand and drive MRP production, shipments pick and issue stock and create the AR invoice (FIN) automatically, and quality or commercial returns are handled with a sales return; internal orders cover in-house and affiliate demand. It also provides overdue follow-up, sales ranking and trend, delivery rate, return rate and sales forecast analysis.

核心流程Core flow

銷售訂單Sales Order（→ MRP → 生產）→ (→ MRP → production) → 出貨單Goods Shipment（揀貨出庫）→ 應收發票（FIN）→ 收款 (picking and issue) → AR invoice (FIN) → collection
退回：Return: 銷退單Sales Return → 退貨入庫 → 沖減應收→ return into stock → reduce AR

出貨以「儲位可用量」（在庫 − 預留）檢核，不足時擋單；出貨確認後自動產生應收發票，作廢時一併回沖分錄。啟用「庫存簡化模式」時，出貨確認即直接出庫，不經倉庫作業揀貨。Shipments are checked against the available quantity of the location (on hand − reserved) and blocked when it is insufficient; confirming a shipment creates the AR invoice automatically, and voiding it reverses the journal entry as well. With "Simplified Inventory Mode" enabled, confirming a shipment issues the stock directly, without warehouse picking.
