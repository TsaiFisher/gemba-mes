盤差分析Stock Variance

路由：/StockVariance  |  盤點差異與調整原因統計Route: /StockVariance  |  Count variance and adjustment reason statistics

彙總盤點差異與各類調整的數量金額，依原因/倉庫統計，追蹤庫存準確率。Summarises count differences and adjustments by quantity and value, grouped by reason and warehouse, to track inventory accuracy.

盤點差異分析畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Count variance analysis screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

查詢Search

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.
