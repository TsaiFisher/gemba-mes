庫存異動帳本Inventory Ledger

路由：/StockLedger  |  品號別期初/進/出/期末帳Route: /StockLedger  |  Opening / in / out / closing ledger by item

以品號為主體的庫存帳：期初量 → 各筆異動 → 期末量與金額，逐筆滾動餘額，供對帳與稽核。A stock ledger per product: opening quantity → each movement → closing quantity and value, with a running balance for reconciliation and audit.

庫存分類帳畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Inventory ledger screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

查詢Search

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.
2
大區間查詢會自動截斷明細筆數，合計仍以 SQL 聚合保持正確。A query over a wide range truncates the detail rows automatically; the totals stay correct because they are aggregated in SQL.
