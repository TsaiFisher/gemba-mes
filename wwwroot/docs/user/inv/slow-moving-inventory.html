呆滯庫存分析Slow-Moving Inventory

路由：/SlowMovingInventory  |  久未異動存貨清單Route: /SlowMovingInventory  |  List of stock with no movement for a long time

依最後異動日找出超過門檻天數未動的存貨及其金額，供促銷、轉用或報廢決策。Finds stock with no movement beyond a threshold number of days, with its value, as input to promotion, reuse or scrapping decisions.

呆滯庫存分析畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Slow-moving inventory screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.
