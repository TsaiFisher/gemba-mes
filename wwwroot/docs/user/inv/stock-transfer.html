同廠調撥單Stock Transfer

路由：/StockTransfer  |  同廠倉間/儲位間移轉Route: /StockTransfer  |  Transfers between warehouses and locations within the same plant

同一廠內跨倉庫或跨儲位的存貨移轉，出入同時過帳，成本不變。Moves stock between warehouses or locations within one plant; both sides post together and the cost is unchanged.

查詢Search

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.

作業流程Process

建立調撥單Create transfer

1
點「新增」開啟側欄，填寫調撥單表頭與明細行後儲存（草稿）。Click "Add" to open the panel, fill in the transfer header and lines, then save (as a draft).
2
點「送審」進入簽核流程；核准後單據自動轉「已確認」。若免簽核，直接點「確認」。Click "Submit" to start the approval flow; the document turns to "Confirmed" once approved. Where no approval is required, click "Confirm" directly.

後續作業Follow-up actions

1
已確認/已過帳單據可點「列印」產生含 QR Code 的正式表單（新分頁開啟）。Confirmed and posted documents can be printed with "Print", producing the official form with a QR code in a new tab.
2
已過帳單據如需沖銷，點「作廢」並填寫原因；系統產生反向庫存/帳務異動。To reverse a posted document, click "Void" and enter a reason; the system creates the reversing stock and accounting entries.

欄位Field說明Guide
品號Item必填Required 要調撥的料件Item to transfer
來源單號Source doc No產生此需求的來源單據；由來源單匯入時自動填寫，用於追溯與回寫Source document that raised this demand; filled in automatically when imported from the source, and used for tracing and write-back
數量 / 單位Qty / UOM必填Required 調撥數量與計量單位。選了品號後，單位只列該品號的庫存單位與查得到換算的單位（預設庫存單位）；存檔時系統依品號主檔換算成庫存數量入帳，查不到換算的單位會擋下並說明。Transfer quantity and unit of measure. Once a product is chosen, the unit list shows only its stock unit and the units that convert to it (stock unit preselected); on save the system converts to the stock quantity from the product master, and a unit with no conversion is rejected with an explanation.
單價Unit Price該批庫存的移動加權平均（AVCO）單位成本，出庫時凍結，不可手動修改Moving average (AVCO) unit cost of that stock, frozen at issue and not editable by hand
撥出倉庫 / 撥出儲位From warehouse / From location必填Required 料件移出的位置；可用量不足者無法確認。也可按欄位旁的放大鏡開啟「選擇庫存」視窗，內含該品號各處的倉庫、儲位、批號、帳上數量、已預留與可用量，選取一列後自動回填倉庫、儲位與批號。若該品號另有保留給訂單／工單的量，視窗上方會以黃色提示列出（這些量不落在儲位上，表格的「可用」沒有扣除；出貨時超過「在手 − 保留」會被擋）。Location the material moves out of; lines without enough available quantity cannot be confirmed. You can also click the magnifier beside the field to open the stock picker, which lists the warehouse, location, lot, on-hand, reserved and available quantity everywhere that item is held; selecting a row fills in the warehouse, location and lot for you. If some of the item is also reserved for orders or work orders, a yellow notice at the top of the window lists it (those reservations are not tied to a location, so "Available" in the table does not deduct them; shipping more than on-hand minus reserved is blocked).
批號Lot No要搬的是哪一個批號的庫存；批號原樣帶到撥入儲位，不會改變。空白＝搬沒有批號的庫存。可用量依「儲位＋批號」判斷：同儲位有別的批號或無批號庫存也不能拿來湊數。完工分箱入庫的每一箱都是一個批號，整箱搬儲位時在這裡填該箱批號（或用「選擇庫存」帶入）；箱子貼有固定 QR 時，直接在這一欄掃箱號，系統會換成該箱目前的批號，並帶出品號、撥出儲位與整箱數量。Which lot of the stock to move; the lot is carried unchanged to the target location. Leave it blank to move stock that has no lot. Availability is checked per location and lot: other lots, or unlotted stock, at the same location cannot make up the quantity. Each box from boxed completion receipt is its own lot, so to move a whole box enter that box's lot here (or pick it with the stock picker); if the box carries a fixed QR label, just scan its box number into this field and the box's current lot, item, source location and full quantity are filled in.
撥入倉庫 / 撥入儲位To warehouse / To location必填Required 料件移入的位置Location the material moves into
撥入批號To lot留空＝批號不變（一般調撥）。填新批號＝把這份庫存換成新批號，數量與單位成本原樣帶過去；主要用在把散裝或混在一起的庫存裝進箱子（箱子畫面的「裝箱」會自動建這種調撥單）。作廢後貨換回原批號。Leave it blank to keep the lot (a normal transfer). Enter a new lot to relabel this stock with it; quantity and unit cost carry over unchanged. It is mainly used to pack loose or shared stock into a box (the Boxes screen's "Pack" creates such a transfer for you). Voiding it puts the stock back under the original lot.
已作業量Put Away倉庫作業實際執行（上架或下架）的累計數量；全部執行完畢後單據自動過帳Cumulative quantity actually processed by warehouse operations (put-away or picking); the document posts automatically once everything is done
備註Notes該明細行的個別說明Notes for this individual line

同廠別內的位置移動不改變存貨總額，僅改變存放位置；跨廠別請改用跨廠調撥。Moving within a plant does not change the total stock value, only where it is held; use a cross-plant transfer between plants.
