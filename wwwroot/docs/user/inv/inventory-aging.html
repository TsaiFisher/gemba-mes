存貨庫齡分析Inventory Aging Analysis

路由：/InventoryAging  |  庫齡分桶（0-30/31-60/61-90/90+）Route: /InventoryAging  |  Aging buckets (0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+)

將存貨依入庫時間分桶統計數量與金額，呈現庫齡結構，輔助存貨健康度管理。Buckets stock by receipt date and totals quantity and value, showing the aging structure as input to inventory health management.

庫齡分析畫面截圖（示意，實際欄位/資料依權限與查詢條件而異）Aging analysis screenshot (illustrative; actual fields and data vary by permission and query criteria)

1
查詢列輸入條件後點「查詢」；欄位標題可點擊排序，清單下方有分頁導航。Enter your criteria on the query bar and click "Search"; column headers can be clicked to sort, and the paging controls sit below the list.

報表欄位Report fields

欄位Field說明Guide
庫齡區間(天)Age Bucket (days)依最後異動日推算的庫齡分桶，固定為 0–30、31–60、61–90、91–180、181–360、360+ 六段（空區間也會列出，金額為 0）Aging buckets derived from the last movement date, fixed at 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, 181–360 and 360+ (empty buckets are still listed, with a value of 0)
庫存筆數Item Count落在該區間的庫存筆數（以儲位／批號為一筆）Number of stock rows in the bucket (one row per location and lot)
庫存金額Inventory Value該區間的庫存金額合計Total inventory value in the bucket
占比Value %該區間金額 ÷ 全部庫存金額；長庫齡占比偏高代表資金積壓Value of the bucket ÷ total inventory value; a high share in the older buckets means capital tied up
